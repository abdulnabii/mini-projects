💡 TOP 3 LESSONS FROM SHIPPING 30 PRODUCTION APPS IN 30 DAYS:

1. Velocity Requires Modular System Design: When shipping daily, you cannot reinvent the wheel. Building reusable UI token systems, standard Gemini SDK wrappers, and shared error boundaries made it possible to build complex systems (like 3D WebGL data clouds and multi-tenant billing) in hours.
2. AI Value Lies in Workflow Context, Not Plain Prompts: Raw LLM output is generic. The apps that stand out are those that integrate AI tightly into domain workflows — like calculating Big-O AST complexity, overlaying GradCAM heatmaps on medical scans, or computing Monte Carlo FIRE probabilities.
3. Polish Is What Makes an App Sticky: Audio feedback, micro-interactions, responsive charts, keyboard shortcuts (⌘K), and instant visual feedback transform a quick demo into a legitimate enterprise tool.
